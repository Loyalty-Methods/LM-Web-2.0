Core Platform

Hyper-Personalization

Stop building separate promotions for every segment. ReactorCX lets business users configure one rule while the engine personalizes spend targets, thresholds, and rewards per member based on behavior and history. Two members can see the same promotion with completely different thresholds. Fewer rules to manage. More relevant offers at the point of interaction.

Schedule Demo

You're Not Building Eight Promotions. You're Building One.

What members see. What targets they receive. What rewards they can unlock. Calculated per member at the point of interaction. One rule does the work of many.

Their view of the world

Query the engine and get a one-shot, summarized view that makes the member feel recognized. Status. Eligibility. What they can get. What they cannot. Why not. Calls to action instead of hidden content.

The moment something happens

Congratulations screens. Instant notifications. Tier upgrades. Streak completions. Events publish the moment conditions are met. Members get satisfaction right away.

Personalized targets, not just offers

Two members see the same streak with different thresholds. Spend targets and rewards tailored to individual appetite. The mechanic is the same. The parameters are unique.

Front-End Personalization

Every Member Sees Their Own Reality

Reduce support calls and confusion. The platform supports real-time queries that return a complete, personalized view for each member. What's my status? What promotions am I eligible for? What rewards can I get? What can't I get, and why not?

One query. Full context. Filtered for that member. The response is not a generic dashboard. It's their view of the world inside your program.

  • Full catalog with eligibility status per member
  • “Why not” messaging explains qualification gaps
  • Unlock criteria creates calls to action
  • Status, progress, and projected outcomes in one response
[App overlay showing streak completion or tier upgrade notification]
Back-End Personalization

Members Know the Moment Something Happens

Drive engagement when it matters most. The platform is event-driven. Anything that happens generates real-time events that you can use to engage members immediately. You achieved a streak. You got a bonus. You hit a new tier.

Events publish the moment conditions are met. Congratulations screens. Instant notifications. App overlays. Members get satisfaction right away instead of waiting for batch jobs.

  • Built-in event types for common loyalty moments
  • Sub-second event publishing
  • Push notifications, SMS, email triggered by events
  • App overlays triggered the moment conditions are met

Every input feeds real-time personalization decisions.

Transaction History

Purchase patterns, frequency, recency, basket composition, category affinity.

Status & Progress

Current tier, points balance, qualification progress, time in program.

Preferences

Zero-party data from surveys, profile completion, stated interests, communication preferences.

Segment Membership

Location & Context

Partner Activity

Linked accounts, partner transactions, coalition activity, cross-brand behavior.
