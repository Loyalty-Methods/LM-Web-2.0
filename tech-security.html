Privacy by Design

Two Deployment Options. You Choose.

ReactorCX operates in two modes depending on your privacy requirements. Store PII directly in the platform with full encryption and access controls. Or operate without PII using tokenized identifiers, with member data stored in your existing systems. MGM runs zero-PII architecture. The platform adapts to your data governance posture.

  • Full PII storage with encryption and access controls
  • Zero-PII option using tokenized identifiers
  • Privacy rights support: access, deletion, rectification, portability
  • PCI scope reduction through tokenization patterns
