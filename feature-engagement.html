FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about engagement, gamification, and member activity tracking.

RCX can track any activity you can send via API or event stream. This includes purchases, app logins, page views, survey completions, profile updates, referrals, social shares, check-ins, event attendance, and custom activities you define. Every activity flows through the same rules engine for consistent evaluation and reward.

Streaks are time-bound engagement mechanics that reward members for completing a series of actions within a defined window. They can be consecutive (visit 5 days in a row) or cumulative (make 3 purchases this month). Streaks can escalate rewards as members progress, creating momentum and habit formation.

Referral programs in RCX can be configured as instant (reward on signup), progressive (reward unlocks as referee hits milestones), or dual-sided (both parties earn). You define the reward structure, eligibility rules, and any limits. The platform tracks referral attribution automatically and handles fraud detection.

Absolutely. RCX treats all activities as first-class engagement events. Survey completions, profile updates, app logins, content consumption, social shares, reviews, and any custom activity can earn points, unlock benefits, or contribute to tier status. The rules engine evaluates non-purchase activities with the same speed and flexibility as transactions.

Activities are processed in real time with sub-second latency. When a member completes an action, the rules engine evaluates it immediately and any resulting rewards, status updates, or notifications are applied before the member leaves the interaction. No batch processing, no delays.
