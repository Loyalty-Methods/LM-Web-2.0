Promotions & Discounting

Launch targeted campaigns with real-time evaluation, best-offer arbitration, and complete margin control.

Campaigns That Scale Without the Complexity

ReactorCX evaluates every discount in real time at checkout. When multiple promotions qualify, the engine selects the optimal combination based on your policies. Sub-second response times at enterprise volume.

Promotion Types

BOGO & Quantity-Based

Buy-one-get-one, mix-and-match bundles, and quantity thresholds. The engine calculates optimal combinations automatically at checkout.

Multiplier Promotions

Layer earning multipliers by tier, day-part, location, or product category. Stack rules are configurable per promotion.

Threshold Spend

Spend-to-unlock promotions with real-time progress visibility across all channels. Members know exactly how close they are.

Time-Limited Offers

Flash promotions with scheduled activation down to hour-of-day. No deployment window required. Publish without service restarts.

Multi-Event Sequences

Streaks, visit counts, and multi-step challenges. Progress tracked in real time across all touchpoints.

Partner-Funded

Co-funded promotions tracked separately for accurate settlement. Clean reimbursement without manual reconciliation.

How It Works

Step 1

Transaction Arrives

Every transaction triggers real-time evaluation. The engine checks member status, basket contents, location, and time of day against all active promotions simultaneously.

Real-Time Evaluation

Step 2

Best Offer Selected

When multiple promotions qualify, the arbitration engine selects the optimal combination. Configurable policies determine whether to maximize member value, protect margin, or balance both.

Arbitration Engine

Step 3

Applied & Logged

The winning offer applies instantly at checkout. Every decision is logged for audit, dispute resolution, and finance reconciliation. Response times stay under 200ms at enterprise scale.
